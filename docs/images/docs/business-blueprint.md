## Step 1: Business Model
- **Model:** Online Store
- **Revenue Stream:** Sells curated mood-boosting lifestyle goods and sensory accessories
- **Differentiation:** A social-first curated commerce platform styled like TikTok Shop
- **One-Sentence Pitch:** Dopamine helps online shoppers boost their mood & discover products they love by offering a fun, personalized, dopamine shopping experience

## Step 2: Customer Segments
1. **Trendy Shopper:** Comes to discover trendy products
Blockers: High prices, boring website, outdated products
2. **Stress Shopper:** Comes to relieve stress by adding items to cart. Blockers: Too many ads, feeling overwhelmed
